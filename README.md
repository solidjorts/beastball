# Beastball

A 3D monster-football game that runs in the browser, on desktop or phone.

Coach the Gloamfield Lanterns against the CPU's Basalt Bruisers over four drives each. Every snap, both sides secretly call a play, then each one-on-one matchup is decided by stats and creature type. Each star also gets one Surge move per drive.

**Play it:** https://solidjorts.github.io/beastball/

## How to play
- Pick a play (keys 1–4 on a keyboard), optionally pick a Surge move, then press **Snap**.
- The tray previews key matchups: 2× super effective, ½× weak, 0× immune.
- Tap the field during a play to fast-forward.
- On 4th down, go for it, punt or kick a field goal.

Built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/) r128. Everything lives in `index.html`.
